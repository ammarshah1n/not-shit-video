## Third-party sources checked

- [CloudAI-X/threejs-skills](https://github.com/CloudAI-X/threejs-skills), commit `0e181866f095b5f06d005133876e926515390d95`: full 13-module reference collection, MIT notice preserved. Files are unchanged except their enclosing paths/names. Upstream examples may mention optional dependencies that are not installed by this pack.
- [remotion-dev/remotion](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion): reused as a pinned runtime dependency, not vendored implementation code. Its company/free licence boundary is documented.
- [remotion-dev/skills](https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills): official additional Remotion guidance; no repository licence was established during inspection, so its text is linked rather than redistributed.
- [mrdoob/three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js) and [pmndrs/react-three-fiber](https://github.com/pmndrs/react-three-fiber): optional 3D runtimes, not unnecessary default dependencies.
- [vercel-labs/skills](https://github.com/vercel-labs/skills): reviewed as an existing installer route. This pack uses a small dependency-free Python installer to keep its complete tools/components together and preserve conflicting installs.
- [bradautomates/claude-video](https://github.com/bradautomates/claude-video): optional video-understanding integration. FFmpeg-based QA works without it.

## Deliberately not published

Raw agent memories/transcripts; personal names and machine paths; real mail/calendar/message data; production auth fixtures; internal application source and unreleased feature contracts; private finished films/captures; provider credentials, signed URLs or account details; downloaded reference-film footage; proprietary system fonts, brand assets and music with unresolved public rights; obsolete real-account recorders; unrelated social-campaign drafts and marketing account lists.

These omissions protect privacy and rights, not a smaller filmmaking scope. No claim is made that a generic pack can reproduce a particular private film without its approved product, assets, brief and creative decisions. The included demo is openly synthetic.
